Your profile

Your CV in plain text or markdown. The morning prep uses it to write answers and STAR stories from your real experience, and to find gaps. It stays private unless you share this dashboard.

Use it in NotebookLM

  1. Open notebooklm.google.com and create a new notebook.
  2. Add source, choose Copied text, and paste.
  3. In the Studio panel, choose Audio Overview, then Customize. Paste this: "Role-play a live job interview. From the first line you are the interviewers and I am the candidate. Ask the questions from the mock interview script one at a time, tell me to pause and answer out loud, then give a strong answer from my CV and push back when an answer is vague or has no numbers."
  4. Use Interactive mode to join the conversation and practise answering out loud.
